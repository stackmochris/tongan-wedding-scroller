# Audio and Voice

Prototype approach for dialogue audio and notes on later pronunciation practice.

## Core Path (Prototype)
- Progression is gated by choice selection only. No voice input, no microphone permission, and no speech recognition in the critical path.
- This keeps orthography barriers low, avoids recognition errors on Tongan, and preserves the simple data-driven system for hand-off.
- All lines are pre-recorded and played on selection or NPC speech.

## Recording Approach
- Single speaker (DR L.V.S) records every line. This ensures consistent pronunciation and natural Tongan cadence across all characters.
- Record in one session with the same mic, distance, and speaking style.
- Character distinction comes first from delivery (pace, energy, formality) and second from light digital processing.
- Moderate pitch and formant shifts are applied for male/female and young/older colouring. Extreme shifts are avoided to prevent artefacts.
- Original clean recordings are kept in the repo so any line can be re-processed without re-recording.
- Test processed lines inside the actual game UI on a phone before locking.

## Post-Prototype Expansion (Pronunciation Testing)
- Optional, non-gating “try saying this” prompts can be added later. They provide feedback but never block progress.
- A separate practice mode outside the critical path can offer light pronunciation practice once reliable Tongan recognition or a simple recording-review pipeline exists.
- Any real-speech gating or scoring remains deferred until after funding and a dedicated accuracy review. It would require new systems (microphone handling, recognition, error recovery) that are intentionally out of scope for the prototype.
- All pronunciation content remains subject to DR L.V.S review.
