## Key principles (summary for quick reference)

1. **Comprehensible input** is necessary (Krashen; widely accepted).
2. **Spaced retrieval** produces durable learning (Ebbinghaus; Cepeda et al. 2006; Kim & Webb 2022).
3. **Output** facilitates noticing and hypothesis testing (Swain).
4. **Vocabulary** needs both quantity of meetings and quality of processing (Nation).
5. **Audiovisual input** shows medium effects; educational video stronger than entertainment (Sutton et al. meta-analysis).
6. **Age**: Grammar-learning ability remains strong until ~17–18 then declines (Hartshorne et al. 2018); adults can still reach high proficiency with sufficient input and practice.
