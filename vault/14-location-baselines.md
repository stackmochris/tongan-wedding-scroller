# Tongatapu Location Visual Baselines

Reference notes for background and environment design. Based on real photographs of the named places used in the core path. Use these as visual ground truth before any stylised (cell-shaded chibi) generation.

## 1. Fuaʻamotu International Airport (Opening & Departure)

- Low, wide terminal building, predominantly white with blue lower walls.
- Large red capital lettering: “FUA'AMOTU INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT”.
- Open paved forecourt, grass verges, scattered palm trees, and light poles.
- Bright tropical sky; simple, recognisable silhouette suitable for exterior arrival and farewell scenes.
- No complex interior required for the prototype.

## 2. Coastal / Inland Road (Fuaʻamotu → Popua / Nukuʻalofa)

- External view only. Scenery rolls past the vehicle.
- Typical elements: coconut palms, roadside villages, occasional church buildings, open fields, and glimpses of coast or lagoon.
- Bright daylight, simple horizontal movement. No interior driving simulation needed.
- Purpose is pointing-language exposure over recognisable Tongatapu landscape.

## 3. Family House — Popua / Nukuʻalofa Residential (Dinner & Wedding Eve)

- Modest single- or lightly raised single-storey home.
- Weatherboard or concrete-block construction, painted white, cream, or light colours.
- Corrugated iron roof (red, grey, or silver).
- Simple veranda, external stairs if raised, open sandy or grassy yard.
- Palm trees and tropical shrubs; water tanks common.
- Neighbouring houses visible but not dense. Suburban-tropical rather than urban.
- Same scene data can serve both dinner and wedding-eve states with different lighting or prop flags.

## 4. Talamahu Market, Nukuʻalofa (Coordination — Supplies)

- Covered market hall with concrete pillars and fluorescent lighting.
- Long tables of fresh produce (tomatoes, root vegetables, greens, fruit).
- Separate handicraft stalls with woven baskets, carved wood, mats, and textiles.
- Busy but orderly interior; strong local identity without requiring photoreal detail.
- Exterior approaches can show simple market frontage and surrounding town buildings.

## 5. Church Grounds — St Paul’s Anglican, Nukuʻalofa (Coordination)

- Cream and yellow church building with red corrugated roof.
- Arched windows, modest Pacific ecclesiastical style, and a cross.
- Low boundary wall, palms, and open grounds.
- Suitable for the polite-address coordination beat. A smaller village church exterior can be substituted if the grandma-visit framing is chosen; keep to one church for the prototype.

## 6. Teufaiva Stadium (Coordination — Sports / Cousin)

- Athletic stadium with green football pitch and blue running track.
- Covered grandstand with red accents.
- Surrounding low buildings, trees, and open sky.
- Clear sports-ground identity for everyday verb and lighter cousin interaction.
- Community-ground simplification is acceptable if full stadium assets are heavy.

## 7. Haʻatafu (Wedding Venue & Optional Beach)

- Coastal setting: sandy beach facing open water, low vegetation, wide sky.
- Suitable for both the wedding venue exterior and any optional beach hangout.
- Simple, bright, and recognisable as a Tongatapu west-coast location.
- Wedding can be staged under a simple canopy or on open grounds near the coast; full indoor venue not required.

## 8. Departure Point

- Reuses Fuaʻamotu Airport exterior (see section 1).
- Farewell staging can be the same forecourt with different character presence and lighting.

## Design Rules for Backgrounds
- Prioritise recognisable silhouettes and key landmarks over photoreal accuracy.
- Cell-shaded chibi style should keep the distinctive colour and form cues (airport signage, market pillars and produce, church roof and cross, stadium track, coastal horizon, residential veranda + palms).
- Areas remain small and discrete. Transitions are simple cuts or short external travel sequences.
- Shared art direction is encouraged where it reduces load (e.g., coastal palette for Haʻatafu wedding and optional beach; house interior/exterior reuse between dinner and eve).

## Hand-off Notes
- Location list is fixed for the prototype.
- New languages or expanded content add dialogue data inside these spaces rather than new maps.
- Real place names and landmark cues provide cultural grounding without requiring accurate 3D recreation.
