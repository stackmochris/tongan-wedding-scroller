## Caption and adaptation rules

The frozen schema has no complex-word or per-item familiarity field. Do not add one. Full English stays in the editorial sidecar. `caption-plan.csv` tells the integrator which nodes are visual-first, first exposure, familiar short line or complex narrative; DR L.V.S overrides complexity after seeing actual Tongan. Simple first-exposure greetings should remain comprehensible in the world and through permitted captioning. Beginner+ does not mean hiding every line. Basic gets at most one extra first-failure model; no endless tutorial. The current engine does not implement this behaviour.

No new target vocabulary enters a revision check. Natural emotional English includes more nuance than the target grammar: DR L.V.S should split/adapt those lines into short familiar clauses, preserve player intent and avoid teaching advanced grammar as a gate. If adaptation adds an unfamiliar target needed by a check, insert a model and successful use earlier in the same scene before approval. No free text, voice scoring, study screen or spaced-repetition system is introduced.
