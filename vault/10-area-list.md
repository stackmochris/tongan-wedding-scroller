# Expected Area List

Minimal set of discrete areas that support the ≤6 hour core path. Locations are mapped to real places on Tongatapu. Each area is small, self-contained, and connected by simple transitions. No open world. All language content remains data-driven.

## Core Path Areas (required)

| # | Area | Real location | Story beat | Primary function | Key language focus | Est. time |
|---|------|---------------|------------|------------------|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Airport exterior | Fuaʻamotu Airport | Opening | Identity choice, first greeting, meet driver | Ko hai? / Ko au… / Malo e lelei | 15–20 min |
| 2 | Coastal / inland road (external view) | Road from Fuaʻamotu toward Nukuʻalofa / Popua | Car ride | Scenery rolling past; pointing language | Sio… / Ko ena… / Ki muʻa | 15–20 min |

**Core total**: ~6 hours maximum when played straight through.

### Location notes
- **Car scene**: Shown from outside the vehicle. Scenery (coconut palms, villages, coast, church) rolls past while the driver points things out. No interior driving simulation required.
- **Church**: St Paul’s Anglican (Nukuʻalofa) works cleanly for a town coordination beat. A smaller village church near Popua is better if the beat is framed as visiting grandma. Either is viable; pick one for the prototype to avoid extra areas.
- **Beach (optional enrichment only)**: Oholei, Pangaimotu, or Haʻatafu beach can host a light cousin hangout if desired. Not required for the core path. Haʻatafu beach pairs naturally with the wedding venue if a single coastal location is preferred.
- **Other named places** (Fasimoeafi, Kolonga, etc.) can appear as passing scenery or later optional content. They are not needed as separate playable areas for the 6-hour spine.

## Optional / Recycling Areas
These reuse existing dialogue data and do not add new vocabulary or extend the critical path.

- Nukuʻalofa market (repeat visit)
- Teufaiva or community ground (repeat sports / cousin hangout)
- Popua family house common area (light additional conversations)
- Haʻatafu or Oholei beach (optional cousin scene)

## Area Design Rules
- Each core area has 1–3 interactable NPCs or objects that gate progress.
- Transitions are explicit (walk to exit, select “travel,” or short external travel sequence).
- No backtracking required for the critical path; optional returns are allowed.
- Visual scope per area: simple background + character sprites sufficient for context. Real place names and recognisable landmarks (airport, market, stadium, coastal road, Haʻatafu) provide grounding without requiring accurate 3D recreation.
- Younger-relative presence is a flag, not a separate area.

## Hand-off Notes
- Area list is fixed for the prototype.
- New languages or expanded content add data inside these areas rather than new maps.
- Popua house scenes (dinner and wedding eve) can share the same underlying scene data with different flags/states.
- Wedding venue and optional beach can share coastal art direction if that reduces asset load.
