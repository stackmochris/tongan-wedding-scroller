# Market (Talamahu Market, Nukuʻalofa)

**Source:** vault/14-location-baselines.md § 4

**Key features:** Covered market hall with concrete pillars and fluorescent lighting. Long tables of fresh produce (tomatoes, root vegetables, greens, fruit). Separate handicraft stalls with woven baskets, carved wood, mats, and textiles. Busy but orderly interior. Strong local identity.
