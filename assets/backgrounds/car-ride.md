# Car Ride (Pointing Sequence)

**Sources:** [Approved visual style](../style-reference.md), vault/14-location-baselines.md § 2.

**Approved presentation:** Exterior side-on light-blue compact car on a horizontal road, occupants visible through windows. Layered scenery rolls past: coconut palms, villages, occasional church, fields and glimpses of coast/lagoon. Bright tropical daylight; same clean outlines, chibi occupants and soft cel shading as dialogue scenes.

**Prompt:** Wide exterior side-on Tongatapu travel scene, cute illustrated 2D chibi art, compact car fully visible with wheels on road, driver looking ahead with hands on wheel and passenger looking out, palms and modest village buildings in separable parallax layers, warm tropical palette. No interior camera, no driving simulation, no baked dialogue or UI. Create character-free scenery plates and separate vehicle/occupant assets for production.

**Learning:** Subtle emphasis can connect a spoken pointing phrase to a landmark; reuse existing dialogue. Tongan content remains subject to DR L.V.S review.
